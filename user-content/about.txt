This is a donation platform for the Happier Lives Institute (HLI).
By clicking on the donate button on our website it will redirect donors
to this separate donation page on which they will select where they want to give,
how much, the split between charities and give us some information about themselves.
Each question should appear one by one, with a subtle fade animation between each step,
and a progress bar should be visible at the top, with a back button always visible and 
a forward button when appropriate.

HLI is an independent research charity that identifies the most 
cost-effective ways to improve human wellbeing. We focus on 
subjective wellbeing — how people actually feel about their lives — 
rather than just income or health outcomes. Our methodology uses
the WELLBY (Wellbeing-Adjusted Life Year), which lets us compare 
the impact of very different interventions on a common scale.

Our donors are typically thoughtful, research-oriented people in the 
effective altruism and effective giving community. They care about 
evidence, want to understand where their money goes, and respond to 
transparency over emotional appeals.

The purpose of this site is to make donating to HLI feel as clear, 
trustworthy, and frictionless as possible. It should feel like a 
natural extension of the HLI brand: calm, rigorous, warm but not 
sentimental. Not a generic charity donation page.

Donors can give one-time or monthly. We accept card payments via 
Stripe. Gift Aid is available for UK taxpayers. Donations can be given
to our recommended charities (StrongMinds, Friendship Bench, Pure Earth
and Taimaka), to HLI or to our Happier Lives Fund which is a fund where 
we use our expert knowledge to disburse in the ways we think best and keep 
10% for HLI. It should be clear the Happier Lives Fund (HLF) is the recommended option.  I want little 
'i' symbols which the donors can hover over to learn more about the options. For donors picking
our recommended charities they should be able to choose a percetange to give
to HLI on a slider which has 0%, 5%, 10%, 15% and 25% with 10% selected by default,
and labelled as recommended. The flow should be donors select which of the options they would
like to give to, then the total amount they would like to give (and whether this is 
one off or monthly), then they should be given sliders which let them decide
the split of these donations between their selected charities. At the bottom
there should be a calculator which provides the number of years of depression averted
by this donation split. I will provide the numbers later but for now assume each dollar
to StrongMinds and Friendship Bench avert 2 years of depression, each dollar to Taimaka 
and Pure Earth avery 1 year, and each dollar to the HLF averts 3.

Donors should be able give in a wide range of currencies.

Key trust signals to include:
- Clear breakdown of what donations fund
- Links to our research and transparency pages at happierlivesinstitute.org
- No percentage taken from donations

Tone: honest, considered, data-driven. Not flashy. Not 
guilt-driven. We respect our donors' intelligence.
